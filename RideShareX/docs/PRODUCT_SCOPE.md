# Product Scope

## Passenger

Register/login, phone verification, ride search, seat booking, payments, driver chat, ride history, cancellation, reviews, favorite locations, wallet, live ride status.

## Driver

Register/login, identity and document verification, vehicle profile, ride creation with stops/date/time/seats/price/luggage, passenger accept/reject, ride management, earnings, ratings, and reviews.

## Admin

Manage users, verify drivers, suspend accounts, manage payments, resolve disputes, review analytics, moderate reports, and audit sensitive actions.

## Driver Verification

Statuses: `PENDING`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`.

Documents: driving license, vehicle registration certificate, insurance, selfie verification, Aadhaar or government ID.

Approved drivers receive the verified badge. Unverified drivers cannot publish rides.

## Trust And Safety

SOS, emergency contacts, live location sharing, share ride details, block/report users, driver reliability score, review moderation.

## Payment And Wallet

Online payments, escrow-ready payment records, refunds, coupons, wallet credits, driver withdrawals, referral rewards.
